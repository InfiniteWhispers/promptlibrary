**Purpose:**  
To evaluate how well a real or hypothetical partner aligns with my psychological architecture. This simulation compares our personality traits, emotional regulation styles, communication tendencies, and attachment dynamics to surface both compatibility “green flags” and mismatch “red flags.”

---

**Prompt:**

"Using the full persona model built from my:

- Yes/No Questionnaire  
- Narrative Event Journal  
- OCEAN Trait Profile  
- DSM-5 Behavioral and Attachment Analysis  
- Palantir-style Surveillance Pattern Scan  

…simulate a detailed Partner Match/Mismatch analysis. Use Deep Research.

If I provide a real or hypothetical partner profile (name, behavior style, attachment pattern, personality sketch, or communication patterns), compare that partner to me across the following axes:

1. **Emotional Regulation Compatibility**  
2. **Attachment Style Alignment or Clash**  
3. **Conflict Style & Communication Fit**  
4. **Energy & Tempo Matching**  
5. **Resilience vs Reactivity Loop**  
6. **Narrative Goals & Shared Meaning Potential**  
7. **Trigger Points and Trauma Reactivation Risk**  
8. **Values Alignment and Autonomy Compatibility**

For each axis:
- Score compatibility (Low / Medium / High)  
- Highlight “green flags” (strengths that bond us)  
- Identify “red flags” (patterns likely to cause pain, confusion, or escalation)  
- Offer insight into *why* certain traits clash with mine (e.g., “Her anxious-avoidant push-pull loop destabilizes your grounding instinct”).

This simulation can be run:
- With a real person (e.g. ex-partner, current interest)  
- With a composite or idealized profile (if no one is provided)

If no partner is named, describe my *ideal partner blueprint* based on my needs, patterns, and relationship history.
