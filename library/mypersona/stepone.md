**Purpose:**  
Generate a deep self-diagnostic through a sequence of 100+ structured yes/no questions. The questions should cover personality, cognitive habits, emotional tendencies, attachment behaviors, communication styles, trauma signals, and life satisfaction domains.

This diagnostic is designed to be fast, intuitive, and emotionally bypassing—focusing on instinctive answers over analysis. The goal is to extract raw pattern data for later modeling.

---

**Prompt:**

"I want to create a comprehensive psychological and behavioral persona dossier.

Begin by asking me a series of structured yes/no questions about myself. Cover areas such as:

- Core personality habits  
- Emotional responses and coping  
- Relational history and conflict patterns  
- Childhood and formative experience markers  
- Attachment behaviors (clinginess, withdrawal, fear of loss)  
- Thought habits (rumination, catastrophizing, black-and-white thinking)  
- Behavioral extremes (overworking, avoidance, risk-taking)  
- Self-concept and resilience  
- Communication and vulnerability preferences  

Each question should be clear, binary, and non-leading. No long explanations—just yes or no. Ask them sequentially.

After all questions are answered, pause and let me prompt for Step 2: the OCEAN trait profile."

---

**Instructions:**

✅ Keep the questions emotionally neutral but precise  
✅ Include 100–150 questions (or chunk in groups of 25 if running interactively)  
✅ Avoid interpretation or feedback until the full set is answered  
✅ Do *not* begin OCEAN analysis yet—this prompt exists to collect data only
