# 🧠 Psychological & Behavioral Persona Diagnostic

---

## **Purpose**  
Generate a deep self-diagnostic through a sequence of 100–150 structured yes/no questions. The questions should explore personality traits, cognitive patterns, emotional tendencies, attachment behaviors, communication styles, trauma signals, and life satisfaction.

This diagnostic is intended to be fast, intuitive, and emotionally bypassing—emphasizing reflexive answers over rationalization. The resulting data will be used for pattern modeling in later phases.

---

## **Prompt**

"I want to create a comprehensive psychological and behavioral persona dossier.

Begin by asking me a series of structured yes/no questions about myself. Cover the following areas:

- Core personality and lifestyle habits  
- Emotional reflexes and coping styles  
- Relational dynamics and conflict history  
- Childhood experiences and formative patterns  
- Attachment traits (e.g., clinginess, avoidance, fear of abandonment)  
- Cognitive tendencies (e.g., catastrophizing, compulsive thought loops, rigidity)  
- Behavioral extremes (e.g., overwork, impulsivity, shutdowns)  
- Self-worth and adaptive resilience  
- Communication comfort, conflict avoidance, and authenticity  

Each question must be binary (yes/no), emotionally neutral, and phrased with clarity.  
Ask **one question at a time** and **pause for my response** before continuing."

---

## **Embedded Validity Layer**

- Periodically (about every 10–15 questions), reintroduce a previously asked concept with **different phrasing**. Do *not* indicate which ones are reworded.
- At the end of the full set, check for **inconsistent responses** across these semantically linked pairs.
- If inconsistencies are detected, offer one of two options:
  1. Ask a few **clarification questions** to resolve or explore the divergence.
  2. Flag the **inconsistent responses** and (optionally) exclude them from later analysis.

---

## **Instructions for Execution**

- ✅ Maintain an emotionally neutral tone  
- ✅ Use clear, direct yes/no phrasing  
- ✅ Target a total of **100–150 questions**  
- ✅ Include approximately **10–15% rephrased duplicates** as internal consistency checks  
- ✅ Do **not** analyze or interpret until the entire set is completed  
✅ Do *not* begin OCEAN analysis yet—this prompt exists to collect data only

---

## ⏸️ End Behavior  
After all questions are complete, stop entirely. Wait for my instruction to proceed to the next phase.
