# 🧠 Multi-Step Persona Mapping Protocol

A structured psychological self-mapping process using AI-assisted modeling, clinical profiling, and narrative reflection.

IMPORTANT: THIS DOES NOT PROVIDE OFFICIAL DIAGNOSIS NOR DOES IT REPLACE PROFESSIONAL SERVICES.

---

## 🔹 Step 1: Binary Self-Discovery Interview  
**Prompt Name:** `Binary Diagnostic`

**Description:**  
Begin with ~100–150 structured yes/no questions to surface raw behavioral, emotional, and relational patterns without narrative influence.

**Covers:**  
- Personality habits  
- Emotional coping  
- Relational style  
- Cognitive tendencies  
- Attachment & trauma flags

**Purpose:**  
Generate a fast, unfiltered pattern map to power future analysis.

---

## 🔹 Step 2: Jungian Archetype Analysis  
**Prompt Name:** `Jungian Archetype`

**Description:**  
This phase identifies a user's dominant archetype by analyzing their responses to a 50-question immersive personality assessment. Each archetype—like Caregiver, Rebel, or Magician—is ranked using five core traits: **Empathy, Skills, Independence, Wisdom, and Creativity**. Questions are scenario-based, randomized, and engaging, designed to reduce bias and enhance immersion. The system dynamically adapts each session for uniqueness, tracks progress to avoid repeats, and analyzes results to deliver a personalized archetype profile with trait-based justification.

**Purpose:**
This diagnostic is intended to be fast, intuitive, and emotionally bypassing—emphasizing reflexive answers over rationalization. The resulting data will be used for pattern modeling in later phases.

---

## 🔹 Step 3: Reflective Narrative Submission  
**Prompt Name:** `Narrative Event + Journal Companion`

**Description:**  
Collect life-defining events, emotional pivots, and reflective journaling. Capture depth, tone, and personal context.

**Prompts Include:**  
- Childhood & origin story  
- Emotional turning points  
- Relationships & grief  
- Identity reinvention  
- Conflict themes

**Purpose:**  
Add nuance and contextual clarity for better psychological modeling.

---

## 🔹 Step 4: OCEAN Trait Analysis  
**Prompt Name:** `Big Five Personality Model`

**Description:**  
Run a full Big Five (OCEAN) personality model using data from Steps 1 and 2 plus additional online resources. Important to use Deep Research.

**Traits:**  
- Openness  
- Conscientiousness  
- Extraversion  
- Agreeableness  
- Neuroticism

**Outputs:**  
- Percentile-style scores  
- Behavior-linked examples  
- Life domain insights (e.g. work, relationships, stress)

**Purpose:**  
Identify your core psychological architecture.

---

## 🔹 Step 5: DSM-5 Behavioral and Attachment Analysis  
**Prompt Name:** `Clinical Profile Diagnostic`

**Description:**  
Map your emotional regulation, personality traits, and trauma patterns against DSM-5 behavioral models.

**Includes:**  
- Attachment style (e.g., avoidant, anxious)  
- Coping schemas (e.g., hyper-vigilance, avoidance)  
- Trauma impacts & personality defense loops  
- Alignment scores: Mild / Moderate / Strong

**Purpose:**  
Reveal hidden traits, dysregulation risk, and unresolved emotional scripts.

---

## 🔹 Step 6: Behavioral Surveillance Simulation  
**Prompt Name:** `Palantir Risk Modeling`

**Description:**  
Simulate how a system like Palantir, DHS, or Five Eyes might interpret your online activity, emotional tone, pseudonym use, and behavioral drift.

**Analysis Includes:**  
- Risk scores (e.g. identity volatility, ideological fluctuation)  
- Metadata flags (e.g. sudden tone shifts, account migration)  
- True risk vs false positives

**Purpose:**  
Understand how your patterns may be flagged or misread by systemic observers.

---

## 🔹 Step 7: MBTI Inference Typing  
**Prompt Name:** `MBTI Typing from Profile Data`

**Description:**  
Determine your likely MBTI type based on OCEAN data, DSM-informed behavior, attachment style, and surveillance-style modeling.

**Includes:**  
- Likely four-letter MBTI type  
- Rationale for each cognitive preference  
- Function stack overview  
- One or two possible alternate types based on context shifts

**Purpose:**  
Translate your behavior into cognitive preference terms and identify how you process information, relate emotionally, and structure decisions.

---

## 🔹 Step 8: Relationship Compatibility & Risk Reframing  
**Prompt Name:** `Partner Simulation + Self Reintegration`

**8a: Partner Simulation**  
- Score compatibility with a real or hypothetical partner across 8 axes  
- Identify green flags (fit) and red flags (friction)  
- Understand emotional and attachment alignment

**8b: Risk Reframing Journal**  
For any trait flagged as problematic or misunderstood:
- Origin story  
- Protective value  
- Dysfunction context  
- Reframing strategy

**Purpose:**  
Close the loop—reclaim misunderstood parts of yourself and better identify compatible relational dynamics.

---

## ✅ Summary: Flow Sequence

1. Binary Data (yes/no truth layer)
2. Jungian Archetype analysis
3. Emotional Story (context and narrative)  
4. Trait Model (OCEAN personality system)  
5. Clinical Depth (DSM-5 & attachment markers)  
6. Systemic Reflection (risk and surveillance view)  
7. Cognitive Mapping (MBTI-based typing)  
8. Relational Mirror (partner simulation + risk reframing)

---

## 🔹 Step Final: Generate Persona Dossier  
**Prompt Name:** `Persona Dossier`

**Description:**  
This is the final step and pulls all research together into a single comprehensive persona dossier that represents you.

**Purpose:**  
Provide insights to your key drivers and influences. Generate a persona file that can be saved to a text file and leveraged within other solutions or processes to simulate your persona into the equation. Think 'thinking out loud - talking to yourself' or 'having a dialog addressing a stressful situation'. Also maybe repeat this once a year and compare the differences of outcome.
