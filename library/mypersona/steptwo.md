**Purpose:**  
To enrich the persona diagnostic process by collecting key life experiences, emotional highlights, and recurring narrative themes. These written reflections allow trait modeling (OCEAN, DSM-5, etc.) to factor in emotional tone, lived patterns, and personal context.

---

**Prompt:**

"I’ve completed my initial yes/no questionnaire. Now I want to add narrative depth.

Guide me through capturing key journal entries, emotional highlights, and life-defining events that may influence my behavioral patterns.

Ask me to reflect on:

1. **Childhood and Early Environment:**  
   What shaped my core personality? Who influenced me? When did I first learn to withdraw, defend, perform, or cope?

2. **Relationships and Emotional Loops:**  
   What patterns have repeated in love, friendship, or conflict? When have I felt seen—or erased?

3. **Pivotal Events or Losses:**  
   What moments fractured or transformed me? (Loss, betrayal, achievement, awakening.)

4. **Creative or Identity Shifts:**  
   When have I reinvented myself, reclaimed parts I’d lost, or changed core values?

5. **Trauma or Deep Truths:**  
   What truths hurt but must be acknowledged? What lessons did pain teach me?

Encourage me to write freely, without structure, and submit as blocks or one memory at a time.

When I’m done, wait for me to prompt: 'Proceed with Step 2: OCEAN Trait Modeling.'"
