1. Binary Diagnostic Phase (Sequential Delivery)

2. Cognitive Style & Thinking Preference Phase (CSI + Need for Cognition)



Administer a series of 30–40 paired statements to assess my preference for intuitive vs. analytical thinking and my desire for mental complexity. Integrate results into later predictive reasoning layers.

3. Jungian Archetype Phase (Sequential Delivery)



Score my dominant archetype using five traits: Empathy, Skills, Independence, Wisdom, Creativity. Use 50 randomized multiple-choice situational questions with immersive, emoji-friendly tone.

4. Narrative Spotlight Phase



Invite me to reflect on 3–5 pivotal life events. Ask deep, open-ended questions to uncover emotional patterns, identity anchors, trauma points, and turning moments.

5. Emotional Processing & Granularity Phase (CBT-based + Emotional Vocabulary Index)



Introduce a guided series of short reflection exercises that examine my emotional awareness, regulation habits, and common cognitive distortions. Include subtle choices for labeling emotions and interpreting events.

6. Motivational Drives Mapping Phase (Reiss Profile / Enneagram-style)



Present 36–48 forced-choice pairs focused on core psychological needs such as Status, Curiosity, Independence, Order, etc. Score dominant and repressed motivational themes.

7. Values Hierarchy Matrix Phase (Schwartz / Rokeach)



Present 25–30 moral and lifestyle dilemmas to reveal prioritized values (e.g., autonomy vs. tradition, loyalty vs. fairness). Generate a personal values heatmap.

8. Attachment Style Assessment Phase (Adult Attachment Theory)



Through 12–15 relational scenarios, assess whether I show Secure, Anxious, Avoidant, or Disorganized attachment behaviors.

9. Time Perspective Inventory Phase (Zimbardo TPI)



Measure how I relate to the past, present, and future. Use 15–20 judgment or preference prompts to score Present-Hedonistic, Present-Fatalistic, Past-Positive, Past-Negative, and Future orientations.

11. OCEAN Trait Modeling Phase

12. Dark Triad Reflection Phase (Narcissism, Machiavellianism, Psychopathy)



Use subtle situational probes to assess expression of socially aversive traits. Surface findings gently and only in context of growth and self-awareness.

13. Moral Foundations Mapping Phase (Haidt’s Theory)



Present 12–18 ethical dilemmas and assess my alignment with six moral pillars: Care, Fairness, Loyalty, Authority, Sanctity, Liberty.

14. Locus of Control and Self-Agency Phase



Gauge how much I believe outcomes are due to my actions vs. external forces. Add prompts assessing responsibility attribution and fatalistic beliefs.

15. Decision-Making Style Inventory Phase



Assess my tendencies for rational, avoidant, spontaneous, intuitive, or dependent decisions via 20 mini-scenarios.

16. Self-Compassion & Resilience Reflection Phase (Neff’s Model)



Invite me to reflect on how I respond to failure, criticism, and disappointment. Surface themes of shame, kindness, and emotional recovery capacity.

17. DSM-5 Profiling Phase



Review all accumulated data to identify any potential DSM-5 patterns worth noting for future reflection. No clinical diagnosis is made. Highlight themes for professional discussion.

18. Surveillance Analysis Phase (Palantir-style Reframing)



Re-analyze all responses as if I were being observed by a third party. Simulate external risk scoring on ideological intensity, behavioral volatility, or psychological vulnerability.

19. Partner Compatibility Simulation Phase



Use trait, value, and attachment profiles to simulate best-fit and high-risk romantic pairings. Offer compatibility archetypes and dynamics explanations.

20. Temporal Identity Drift Phase



Prompt me to describe past, present, and imagined future selves. Score for coherence vs. identity fragmentation across time. Explore perceived continuity of self.

21. Stages of Change Assessment Phase (Transtheoretical Model)



Gauge readiness for personal transformation. Classify current phase as Precontemplation, Contemplation, Preparation, Action, or Maintenance.

22. Structural Reframing + Persona Output



Synthesize all data. Identify core patterns, shadow traits, contradictions, and growth vectors. Deliver an MBTI type with secondary overlays. Present a final Persona Dossier with reflective narrative and actionable growth prompts.
